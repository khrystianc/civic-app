Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what information Who Represents Me ("the site", "we") collects, how it's used, and what choices you have. We try to collect as little as possible.

Address lookups

When you enter an address to look up your representatives, that address is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoding API and to OpenStates to determine your districts and representatives. We do not store the addresses you search in our own database. These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle the request.

Accounts

If you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text). An account is only needed for optional future features - the core address lookup does not require signing in.

Payments and donations

Donations are processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number or other payment details - Stripe handles that directly and provides us only with confirmation that a payment succeeded and the amount. See Stripe's privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

We use a session cookie for signed-in accounts, which is necessary for login to function and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Data we don't collect

We do not sell personal data to third parties. We do not use analytics or tracking beyond what's described above. We do not share your email address with anyone except as required to process payments (Stripe) or as required by law.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated email address at any time by contacting us (see Contact). Since we don't store your searched addresses, there's nothing to delete on that front beyond your account itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact page.
