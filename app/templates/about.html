About Who Represents Me

Who Represents Me is a free tool that answers a question a lot of people can't: who actually represents them, at every level of government, and what does that person's office actually control? Enter your address and see your federal, state, and local representatives in one place, along with a plain-language explanation of what each office is responsible for - no spin, just facts.

Why this exists

Most people can name their president. Far fewer can name their state representative, their county commissioner, or explain the difference between what a city council controls versus a school board. That gap makes it hard to know who to actually contact when something in your community needs to change. This tool closes that gap with one address lookup.

Where the data comes from

Address-to-district lookups use the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoding API. State legislator data comes from OpenStates, a nonpartisan open data project. Local government data (county commissioners, city councils, school boards) is manually researched and verified against official government sources, starting with a pilot coverage area in Oregon.

How this is funded

This site is free to use, with no features locked behind a paywall. It's supported by advertising and optional pay-what-you-want donations from users who find it valuable. Neither has any influence on what data is shown or how it's presented.
